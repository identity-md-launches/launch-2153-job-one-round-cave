## Shared handoff

The copied census algorithm lives at `shared/merge-dependencies/components.py`; `CENSUS-PROVENANCE.json` records the two path-only adaptations and exact source/copy hashes. This makes the combined edit's change in choice count visible even when the merged catalog remains compatible. Equal counts do not imply equal sets; the existing shared `merge_equivalence.py` answers that separate question.

## Scope and coins

Each goal has a working bounded version and remains distinct from the other three. The broad wording would exceed 21 workers if treated as complete semantic merging, full Ethereum consensus, a truth engine or an unrestricted package manager. Keep line 1 to bounded UTF-8 trees and explicit human decisions; line 2 to supported proofs under caller-authenticated checkpoints and reviewed layouts; line 3 to bounded offline collections with byte-identified citations; and line 4 to finite numeric catalogs with explicit search budgets. No goal replacement is proposed.

`COINS.md` says **none**. These offline/read-only capabilities do not need a token or Uniswap v4 hook. ZTO first and IMD second already serve the real historical chain examples; no coin or Sepolia deployment order is justified.
